**[KG4J — Knowledge Graphs for Justice](/eng/kg4j/)** starts in March 2026 for **four years**.

Funded by BELSPO, it explores knowledge graphs and artificial intelligence to connect forensic and criminological data at the **[NICC](/eng/nicc/)**.
