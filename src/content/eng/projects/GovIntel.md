**GovIntel — Governance & Intelligence** is an internal [NICC](/eng/nicc/) project dedicated to developing and using an **information system based on the administrative data of national forensic DNA databases**.

## Research question

How can the administrative data of DNA databases support their operation, research and forensic intelligence? GovIntel aims to organize this information in a system that allows its exploration and analysis.

## My contribution

I **initiated the project and secured €30,000 in internal funding** to engage a consultant to support its technical aspects. I lead the project, aiming to connect the institution’s operational needs with scientific uses of its data.

## Intended uses

The information system pursues three complementary objectives:

- facilitate **data exploration and operational reporting**;
- support research on **recidivism and criminal careers**;
- develop the use of data for **forensic intelligence**.

GovIntel extends my work on DNA data at the NICC by providing a framework for structuring and using it that meets the institution’s needs.
