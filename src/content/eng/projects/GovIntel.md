## Research question

How can the administrative data of national DNA databases support their operation, research and forensic intelligence?

## Objectives

The internal **[NICC](/eng/nicc/)** project aims to develop an information system enabling:

- data exploration and operational reporting;
- the study of recidivism and criminal careers;
- data analysis for forensic intelligence.

A consultant supports the technical aspects.
