**IIHA — It Is Happening Again** is a project conducted at the [NICC](/eng/nicc/) to study recidivism and criminal careers using digital criminal justice archives.

## Research question

How can administrative data collected for the operation of the justice system be used to study criminal justice trajectories? The project aimed to bring together complementary sources as a basis for studying recidivism and criminal careers.

## My contribution

I **co-authored the funding proposal** and contributed to **scientific leadership** as **co-principal investigator** (*co-PI*). My contributions include developing an **integrated historical database** and presenting methods for structuring data to study criminal justice trajectories.

## Data and methods

The work used data from the **Central Criminal Record** and the **SIDIS-Griffie prison database**. These sources provide complementary information on convictions and prison trajectories.

Integrating them involves understanding their structure, documenting their information and organizing relationships between data. The work notably explored a **graph-based integrated historical database** to support analysis of criminal careers and recidivism.

## Works and outputs

Outputs associated with the project include:

- the report **[La base de données du Casier judiciaire central](/eng/work/#publication-huynen-et-al-2024-tech-report-iiha-cjcs-documentation)**;
- the report **[SIDIS-Griffie databank](/eng/work/#publication-maes-et-al-2024-tech-report-iiha-sidis-greffe-documentation)**;
- the presentation **[The development of an Integrated Historical Database to study Criminal Careers and Recidivism](/eng/work/#publication-jeuniaux-et-al-2022-talk-iiha-ihd)**, on the development of the integrated historical database.
