## Research question

How can digital archives collected for justice administration be used to study criminal justice trajectories?

## Data and methods

The project at the **[NICC](/eng/nicc/)** connected data from the **Central Criminal Record** and the **SIDIS-Griffie prison database**. The work explored a graph-based integrated historical database linking convictions and prison trajectories.
