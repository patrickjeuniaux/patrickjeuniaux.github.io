## Research question

How can cooperation between institutions exchanging forensic DNA data be improved and those exchanges used for research? **PIES** (*Prüm Implementation, Evaluation, and Strengthening of Forensic DNA Data Exchange*) combined the study of exchange practices with analysis of cross-border crime.

## Consortium

Seven institutions in four countries: the **[NICC](/eng/nicc/)** in Belgium; the Sous-Direction de la Police Technique et Scientifique in France; the Nederlands Forensisch Instituut, the Nederlands Studiecentrum Criminaliteit en Rechtshandhaving and Leiden University in the Netherlands; Northumbria University and Sustainable Criminal Justice Solutions in the United Kingdom.

**Europol** participated as a partner without receiving funding.
