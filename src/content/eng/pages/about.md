## Current roles

- **[NICC](/eng/nicc/)**: leadership of the **[DAT](/eng/dat/)** laboratory and the role of Chief Data Officer.
- **[KG4J](/eng/kg4j/)**: principal investigator of the knowledge graphs for justice project.
- **[UCLouvain](/eng/uclouvain/)**: research collaborator at the Louvain Research Institute in Management and Organizations (LouRIM) and member of the Louvain Interaction Laboratory.

## Training and background

- **[UCLouvain](/eng/uclouvain/)**: degree in psychology and educational sciences, specialising in cognitive psychology; postgraduate diploma in statistics.
- **[KU Leuven](/eng/ku-leuven/)**: further training in artificial intelligence.
- **[University of Memphis](/eng/uofm/)**: doctorate in experimental psychology, specialising in psycholinguistics, on learning relationships between forms and meanings.
- **[Université Laval](/eng/ulaval/)**: postdoctoral research on cognition and decision-support systems, with DRDC and Thales.
- **[University of Pisa](/eng/unipi/)**: postdoctoral research in computational linguistics, on distributional semantic models.
- **Data Strategy**: continuing education certificate from the Haas School of Business at the University of California, Berkeley, in a programme offered by Emeritus.

## Methods

Experimental, statistical and computational methods; development with Python, R, SQL and Neo4j. Processing prioritises data quality, traceability and documentation.

## Teaching and supervision

Teaching research methodology and cognitive processes at Memphis; supporting courses in digital transformation, programming and information systems at UCLouvain. Supervision of internships in criminology, psychology and biological engineering.
