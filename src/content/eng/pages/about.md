## Current responsibilities

I lead the **[Data, Artificial Intelligence & Transformation (DAT)](/eng/dat/)** laboratory at Belgium’s **[National Institute of Criminalistics and Criminology (NICC)](/eng/nicc/)**. I also serve as **Chief Data Officer**. My work covers data governance, information management, artificial intelligence and support for the institute’s transformation.

I am the **principal investigator and author of the proposal** for **[Knowledge Graphs for Justice (KG4J)](/eng/kg4j/)**, a project dedicated to integrating and using justice data through knowledge graphs. This work builds on my research into forensic and criminological data, recidivism and criminal careers.

I am also a **research collaborator** at the Louvain Research Institute in Management and Organizations (**LouRIM**) at **[UCLouvain](/eng/uclouvain/)** and a member of the **Louvain Interaction Laboratory**. These collaborations focus particularly on information systems, knowledge graphs and human–computer interaction.

## Background

My professional background has involved work in Belgium, the United States, Canada and Italy.

I studied **cognitive psychology** and undertook further training in **statistics** at **[UCLouvain](/eng/uclouvain/)**, as well as further training in **artificial intelligence** at **[KU Leuven](/eng/ku-leuven/)**.

I earned a **doctorate in experimental psychology, specialising in psycholinguistics**, at the **[University of Memphis](/eng/uofm/)**. My dissertation examined the role of feedback in learning relationships between forms and meanings.

I held two **postdoctoral positions**: one at **[Université Laval](/eng/ulaval/)**, on cognition and the evaluation of decision-support systems in complex environments, in collaboration with **[DRDC](/eng/drdc/)** and **[Thales](/eng/thales/)**; and another in **computational linguistics** at the **[University of Pisa](/eng/unipi/)**, on distributional semantic models and knowledge representation.

At the **[NICC](/eng/nicc/)**, my work concerns forensic DNA databases, recidivism, criminal careers and the integration of administrative data. In particular, I coordinated the European **[PIES](/eng/pies/)** project.

I also completed training leading to a **continuing education certificate in Data Strategy** from the **Haas School of Business at the University of California, Berkeley**, in a programme offered by **Emeritus**. This training covers data strategy, data governance and the development of organisational capabilities.

## Areas of work

My work currently lies at the intersection of several areas:

- **criminal justice data** (forensic DNA intelligence, recidivism, criminal careers, etc.);
- **knowledge graphs** and graph databases;
- **data governance** and the considered use of AI in public institutions.

This work forms part of a broader reflection on how information systems can support decision-making, knowledge transfer and organisational transformation.

## Methods and tools

I use experimental, statistical and computational methods. My main technical tools are **Linux**, **Bash**, **Python**, **R**, **SQL**, **Neo4j**, **Jupyter** and **LaTeX**. I place particular emphasis on data quality, traceability of processing and clear documentation.

## Selected milestones

My work has addressed language acquisition, computational linguistics, distributional semantic models, criminal justice databases, radicalisation, recidivism and knowledge graphs. I have also taught at university and contributed to preparing or managing several funded projects.

## Teaching and supervision

At the **[University of Memphis](/eng/uofm/)**, I designed and taught courses in **research methods in psychology** and **thinking and cognitive processes**. These courses combined theoretical concepts with research and data-analysis exercises.

At the Louvain School of Management at **[UCLouvain](/eng/uclouvain/)**, I supported courses in **digital transformation**, **programming** and **information systems**. My role included running practical sessions, supporting students and assessing their work, using tools such as Python, Odoo, Access, Excel and VBA.

I also supervised **internships in criminology, psychology and biological engineering**, addressing forensic DNA databases, the reintegration of returnees, radicalisation and discourse analysis.
