The collaboration with **Thales**, conducted during the postdoctoral appointment at **[Université Laval](/eng/ulaval/)**, evaluated a mobile situation-awareness support system in a simulated combat environment.

The aim was to study the use of complex information in understanding situations and coordinating operators.
