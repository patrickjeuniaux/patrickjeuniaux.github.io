## Data and organisation

The **[DAT](/eng/dat/)** laboratory supports data and knowledge management, the development of analytical tools and changes in working practices.

Patrick Jeuniaux leads the laboratory and serves as **Chief Data Officer**. This role includes overseeing audits and preparing a governance strategy and data inventory, with general management and those responsible for IT, data protection and risk management.

## Research and projects

- **[KG4J](/eng/kg4j/)**: integration of forensic and criminological data using knowledge graphs and AI.
- **[IIHA](/eng/iiha/)** and **[CReCC](/eng/crecc/)**: recidivism and criminal careers studied through justice data.
- **[GovIntel](/eng/govintel/)**: information system for the administrative data of DNA databases.
- **[PIES](/eng/pies/)**: European cooperation on forensic DNA data exchange.
- **[REGUIDE](/eng/reguide/)**: reintegration pathways of people returning from conflict zones.
