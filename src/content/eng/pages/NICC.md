At the **National Institute of Criminalistics and Criminology (NICC)**, I hold responsibilities for data and organisational transformation while continuing my research activities.

## Leading the DAT laboratory

I lead the **[Data, Artificial Intelligence & Transformation (DAT)](/eng/dat/)** laboratory at the NICC. Its work brings together data, information and knowledge management, data science and artificial intelligence, and organisational transformation.

Working with scientific, operational and support teams, I contribute to developing the institute’s capabilities, improving information flows and supporting decision-making and knowledge transfer. The **[DAT laboratory overview](/eng/dat/)** describes these responsibilities in detail.

## Chief Data Officer

I serve as **Chief Data Officer**. My work includes supervising external audits of data governance and artificial intelligence readiness, as well as drafting a proposed governance strategy and a data inventory plan.

This work is carried out in collaboration with senior management and those responsible for IT, data protection and risk management. It aims to improve the quality, traceability and reuse of the NICC’s information resources.

## DNA data and forensic intelligence

My forensic research concerns the **operation and cost of DNA analysis**, the effectiveness of forensic expertise and the use of genetic databases. In particular, I study how matches between DNA profiles can be represented as networks to analyse connections between cases and criminal trajectories.

I **coordinated the European [PIES](/eng/pies/) consortium**, dedicated to exchanging forensic DNA data under the Prüm framework. I also initiated and led **[GovIntel](/eng/govintel/)**, an information-system project for the national DNA databases, intended to facilitate their exploration and support research and forensic intelligence.

## Recidivism and criminal justice trajectories

Together with colleagues, I **co-founded the [CReCC](/eng/crecc/) research unit**, dedicated to recidivism and criminal careers. My criminological work concerns criminal justice data, conviction and imprisonment trajectories, radicalisation and reintegration.

In **[IIHA](/eng/iiha/)**, I co-authored the funding proposal and contributed to scientific leadership as **co-principal investigator**. My contributions include developing an integrated historical database from digital criminal justice archives. I also contributed to **[REGUIDE](/eng/reguide/)** research on the reintegration of people returning from conflict zones.

## Knowledge graphs and methods

I am the **principal investigator and author of the proposal** for **[KG4J — Knowledge Graphs for Justice](/eng/kg4j/)**. This project explores how knowledge graphs and artificial intelligence can connect heterogeneous sources and strengthen the analysis of forensic and criminological data.

My work combines **database design**, record linkage to identify the same entities in different sources, statistical analysis and graph methods. I use **Python, R, SQL, Jupyter and Neo4j**, with particular attention to documentation and reproducibility of processing. These quantitative approaches are also complemented by fieldwork and interviews.

## Supervision and related work

I have supervised internships in **criminology, psychology and biological engineering**, addressing DNA databases, the reintegration of returnees, radicalisation and discourse analysis.

The following works provide an introduction to these contributions:

- **[Establishing Networks in a Forensic DNA Database to Gain Operational and Strategic Intelligence](/eng/work/#publication-jeuniaux-et-al-2016-networks)**, on using DNA matches as networks;
- **[The development of an Integrated Historical Database to study Criminal Careers and Recidivism](/eng/work/#publication-jeuniaux-et-al-2022-talk-iiha-ihd)**, on structuring historical criminal justice data;
- **[Vision stratégique « data » : gouvernance des données, gestion de l’information et intelligence artificielle](/eng/work/#publication-jeuniaux-et-al-2026-data-strategy2-fr)**, presenting a collective reflection on the institute’s information capabilities.
