I completed a **postdoctoral appointment** in Sébastien Tremblay’s **Cognition–Distribution–Organisation–Technologies (CO-DOT)** laboratory, at the School of Psychology of **Université Laval**, in Québec City.

## Cognition and decision support

My research examined how operators understand complex situations, coordinate their actions and use decision support tools. It combined experiments in simulated environments with the analysis of behaviour and action sequences.

I worked in particular on:

- the evaluation of a decision support system for complex military operations, in collaboration with **[DRDC](/eng/drdc/)**;
- the analysis of a simulation in which firefighting units had to coordinate their interventions;
- the evaluation of a mobile situation awareness support system in a three-dimensional simulated combat environment, in collaboration with **[Thales](/eng/thales/)**.

## Methods and collaborations

My work included conducting experiments, statistical data analysis and the study of action sequences to understand decision-making processes. I used **MySQL** and **SPSS**, among other tools, to organise and analyse the data.

The collaborations with DRDC and Thales formed part of this postdoctoral research. They provided a setting for studying how information systems contribute to understanding situations and coordinating operators.

## Related work

The article **[A cognitive prosthesis for complex decision-making](/eng/work/#publication-tremblay-et-al-2017-prosthesis)** presents research on cognitive support for complex decision-making. Another contribution, **[A multi-perspective approach to the evaluation of a portable situation awareness support system in a simulated infantry operation](/eng/work/#publication-tremblay-et-al-2011-awareness)**, examines the evaluation of a mobile situation awareness support system.
