Patrick Jeuniaux’s postdoctoral work in Sébastien Tremblay’s **Cognition–Distribution–Organisation–Technologies (CO-DOT)** laboratory addressed decision-making and coordination in complex environments.

## Research

Experiments in simulated environments and behavioural analyses addressed:

- a decision-support system for military operations, with **[DRDC](/eng/drdc/)**;
- coordination between firefighting units;
- a mobile situation-awareness support system in simulated combat, with **[Thales](/eng/thales/)**.
