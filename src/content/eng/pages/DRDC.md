The collaboration with **DRDC**, conducted during the postdoctoral appointment at **[Université Laval](/eng/ulaval/)**, evaluated a decision-support system for complex military operations.

The work combined experiments in simulated environments with behavioural analysis to understand how operators interpret information and coordinate their actions.
